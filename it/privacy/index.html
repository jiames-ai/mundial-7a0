Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

7A0 game is a free browser game for building a World Cup XI and simulating a draft result. You can play without creating an account or sending us your name, email address or profile details.

What stays on your device

The core game runs in your browser. It may save language, game mode, formation or stream-view preferences in local storage so the next round feels familiar. Clearing your browser data removes those preferences.

Shared cards and technical logs

If you create a share link, the link may encode your draft seed, formation and selected player references so the card can be opened again. Hosting and security systems may also keep short technical logs such as IP address, URL, browser type and error timing.

Cookies and ads

If advertising or analytics are enabled, third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits, prevent abuse and personalize ads according to their own policies. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page. We may need the relevant URL or share code to understand a request, but please avoid sending sensitive personal information.
